Important Notes — Local Students

  1. Acceptance Fee: All newly admitted 100 Level students are required to pay a non-refundable acceptance fee of ₦100,000.
  2. Academic Fees Coverage: Academic fees cover tuition, examination and registration charges, and student accommodation. Feeding and personal expenses are not included.
  3. Clinical Fee: Nursing Science students from 200 Level to 500 Level and Medical Laboratory Science students from 300 Level to 500 Level are required to pay a clinical fee of ₦500,000 per student per session in addition to applicable academic fees.
  4. Payment Schedule: 50% of academic fees is payable before resumption and registration. The remaining 50% is payable on or before resumption for the second semester.
  5. Parents’ Forum Due: ₦10,000 through the University portal.
  6. Additional Charges: Hostel maintenance, student study fees, medical fees, school materials and other applicable levies and charges are payable separately where applicable.

Important Notes — International Students

  1. Acceptance Fee: $500 for newly admitted 100 Level students.
  2. Clinical Fee: Nursing Science students from 200 Level to 500 Level and Medical Laboratory Science students from 300 Level to 500 Level are required to pay a clinical fee of $1,000 per student per session in addition to applicable academic fees.
  3. Payment Schedule: 50% before resumption and registration. The remaining 50% on or before resumption for the second semester.
  4. Parents’ Forum Due: $100 through the Bursary Department.
  5. Additional Charges: Academic fees do not include applicable additional charges.
  6. Personal Expenses: International students are responsible for feeding, transportation, personal expenses and other costs not specifically covered by academic fees.

General Fee Information

The fee structure distinguishes between Local Student Fees in Nigerian Naira and International Student Fees in United States Dollars.

All fees are applicable to the 2026/2027 academic session and remain subject to the University’s applicable policies, approved charges and any official amendments issued during the academic session.

Additional charges such as hostel maintenance, student study fees, medical fees, school materials and other applicable levies are not included in the academic fees stated in the table and may be billed separately.
